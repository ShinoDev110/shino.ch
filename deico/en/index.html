Services

From one-off to series

Meeting your specifications is our highest priority.

  • CNC turning

    We produce your turned components as CNC contract work, manufactured from your 3D data or drawings. We also make spare parts from a sample.

  • CNC 5-axis milling

    We produce your milled components as CNC contract work. One of our particular strengths is manufacturing complex, demanding parts in low volumes.

  • Development and design

    We design precision components and produce all the associated manufacturing documentation. We also develop complete assemblies, and calculate and simulate mechanical parts in house.

From a single source

What sets us apart

Manufacturing expertise in detail

  • Difficult-to-machine materials

    We also machine difficult materials such as nickel-based alloys (Hastelloy, Inconel) and titanium alloys.

  • Cost-effective series

    Our automated production machines let us manufacture series cost-effectively.

  • Surface measurement and reporting

    Surfaces on parts for the food and pharmaceutical industries and for medical technology must meet high requirements. Modern measuring equipment lets us measure and document them.

  • Our own delivery service

    Your components matter to us. That is why we deliver them personally with our own delivery service.

  • Laser marking

    Through our partner companies we produce a wide range of laser markings.

  • Surface treatment

    Through our partner companies a wide range of surface coatings is possible, for aluminium for example anodising, hard anodising or the Hartematalieren process.

Special solutions

When off-the-shelf will not do

  • Robot accessories

    We develop and produce custom robot accessories such as gripper jaws, adapters for various grippers and parts nests for special workpieces.

  • Sheet metal designs

    We create sheet metal designs and their flat patterns in our CAD system. The sheet metal itself is manufactured by our partner companies.

  • Assembly

    We assemble your fixtures, sub-assemblies, plant and machines.

At home in a wide range of industries
