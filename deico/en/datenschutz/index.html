Website analysis with Google Analytics

This website uses Google Analytics 4, a web analytics service provided by Google LLC, after obtaining the user's consent. The responsible entities are Google Ireland Limited, Google Building Gordon House, 4 Barrow St, Dublin, D04 E5W5, Ireland, for users from the EU/EEA and Switzerland. Google Analytics 4 applies IP anonymisation by default, meaning your IP address is truncated immediately when you interact with this website. Only in rare cases is your full IP address transmitted to a Google server based in the USA and truncated there. Google states that the IP address transmitted via Google Analytics is never combined with other Google data. Google Analytics 4 is only activated with your consent via the cookie banner and can be revoked at any time via the cookie settings.

The data processed includes: usage data such as page views, website interaction, general location and visit duration; and communication data such as truncated IP addresses, user agent, language settings and screen resolution. The purpose of this processing is for Google to evaluate the use of your website on your behalf, compile reports on activity and analyse website performance. This processing is lawful on the basis of your consent pursuant to Art. 6(1)(1)(a) GDPR and § 25(1)(1) TDDDG.

Google Ireland Limited, Google LLC and Alphabet Inc are the recipients of this data. Your website usage information is generally sent to a Google server in the USA, an action legitimised by the European Commission's recent adequacy decision of 10 July 2023, which recognises Google LLC's certification under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Regarding data storage, any data linked to cookies is automatically deleted after a certain period, monthly for data that has reached its retention period, and a maximum of 2 years for Google Analytics cookies.

For further information on the Google Analytics terms of use and on Google's privacy policy, please visit policies.google.com and support.google.com/analytics/answer/12017362.

Google Analytics uses so-called cookies, text files stored on the visitor's computer that enable an analysis of the visitor's use of the website. The information generated by the cookie about the visitor's use of this website (including the truncated IP address) is generally transmitted to a Google server and stored there.

Google Analytics is used on this website exclusively with the _anonymizeIp() extension. This extension ensures anonymisation of the IP address by truncation and rules out direct identification of an individual. Through this extension the IP address is truncated beforehand by Google within member states of the European Union or in other states party to the Agreement on the European Economic Area. Only in exceptional cases is the full IP address transmitted to a Google server in the USA and truncated there. The IP address transmitted by the browser in the context of Google Analytics is not merged with other Google data.

On behalf of the website operator, Google will use the information collected to evaluate use of the website, compile reports on website activity and provide the operator with further services relating to website and internet use (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). The legitimate interest in the processing lies in optimising this website, analysing its use and adapting its content. Users' interests are sufficiently safeguarded by pseudonymisation.

Google LLC. is certified under the so-called Privacy Shield (list entry here) and on this basis guarantees an adequate level of data protection. Data sent and linked to cookies, user identifiers (e.g. User-ID) or advertising IDs is automatically deleted after 50 months. Deletion of data that has reached its retention period takes place automatically once a month.

Collection by Google Analytics can be prevented by adjusting the cookie settings for this website. The collection and storage of the IP address and of the data generated by cookies may also be objected to at any time with effect for the future. The corresponding browser plug-in can be downloaded and installed at the following link: tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

The visitor can prevent collection by Google Analytics on this website by clicking the following link. An opt-out cookie is then set which prevents future collection of data when visiting this website.

Further information on Google's use of data, together with settings and objection options, can be found in Google's privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy) and in the settings for the display of advertising by Google (adssettings.google.com/authenticated).

Spam protection with Google reCAPTCHA

We have implemented Google reCAPTCHA in forms on our website in order to check whether the data entered in the forms originates from human visitors or from machines or automated programs, also known as bots. This tool automatically analyses the behaviour of website visitors as soon as they interact with the website, using various information such as IP address, time spent and mouse movements. The data processed includes usage data such as the page accessed, the date and time of access and mouse movements, as well as communication data such as IP addresses, browser type and operating system. This processing concerns website visitors and users of online services. The main purpose is to prevent misuse of our contact form and thereby increase security. The legal basis is our legitimate interest pursuant to Art. 6(1)(1)(f) GDPR. The data recipients are Google Ireland Ltd, established at Google Building Gordon House, Barrow St, Dublin 4, Ireland, (policies.google.com/privacy) and our website hosting provider. While your personal data is processed within the EU, the retention period for the processed data is determined by Google Ireland Limited. Further information can be found in the Google reCAPTCHA privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

Website analysis (Creator statistics)

When you visit our website, we collect information about your usage through a web analytics tool provided by our hosting service. This tool collects and combines your IP address and your user agent, truncates them and stores this data using a hash function. Through this process a visitor identifier is created, encrypted with a randomly generated value (SALT) that changes every 24 hours. This method ensures that your IP address cannot be reconstructed from the stored visitor identifier, so your anonymity is preserved. Furthermore, we do not merge this information with other data and it is stored only on the hosting provider's server.

We also process web analytics data, HTTP data and web analytics profile data. The web analytics tool we use creates and stores a web analytics profile containing details of your use of the website, such as page views, visit frequency, time spent on each page and the user agent of your device. This includes usage data (such as pages visited and access times) and communication data (such as browser type, operating system and IP addresses).

By processing this data we aim to analyse user behaviour in aggregated form in order to improve the presentation and content of our website. The legal basis for this processing is our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), in particular in carrying out web measurement to improve our products and our website.

The data collected is shared with our website hosting provider and processed within the EU.
